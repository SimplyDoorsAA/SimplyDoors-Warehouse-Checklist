SimplyDoors Incident Report

Provide a detailed, factual account of the incident. Include only objective observations, avoiding opinions or assumptions. Document who was involved, what occurred, where and when it happened, and any relevant statements made by those involved.
Describe any immediate actions taken to address the incident, including any safety measures, removal from duty, counseling, or other corrective actions.
Identify the underlying cause(s) of the incident, including any contributing factors such as equipment, procedures, training, communication, environmental conditions, or human error.
Describe the actions that could have prevented the incident and the steps that will be taken to reduce the likelihood of a similar incident occurring in the future.
